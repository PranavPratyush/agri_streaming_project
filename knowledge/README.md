# Agronomy Knowledge Base & Retrieval Documents

**Directory:** `knowledge`  
**Purpose:** Agronomic reference documents, FAO-56 crop water guidelines, ICAR advisory packages, and vector store indices.  
**Scheduled Stage:** Stage 15  

## Policy & Licensing
- Do NOT place copyrighted, untraced, or unverified documents here without explicit permission.
- Store source document metadata in `knowledge/manifest.json` including author, title, publication year, regional applicability, and DOI/URL.
- Vector embeddings and Chroma storage are managed under `knowledge/chroma_db/` (excluded from git).
