# Agronomic Safety & Advisory Guardrails — AgriKafka-Agent

AgriKafka-Agent generates actionable advice for real farmers. Inappropriate advice (e.g. chemical overdosing, premature watering, misdiagnosed pathogen blight) can cause severe economic and environmental harm.

---

## 1. Absolute Prohibitions
The system and its AI agents are strictly forbidden from:
- **Chemical Dosages:** NEVER prescribe exact pesticide, fungicide, herbicide, or chemical fertilizer mixing concentrations (e.g., "mix 2.5 ml/L of Mancozeb"). Advise only broad cultural management practices, official IPM guidelines from ICAR/KAU, or direct the farmer to a local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).
- **Unsupported Disease Diagnoses:** NEVER declare definitive fungal, bacterial, or viral disease presence solely from ambient weather data or modelled soil moisture without in-field visual or laboratory pathology confirmation. Only environmental *risk windows* (e.g., "high humidity and mild temperatures favor fungal spore germination") may be flagged.
- **Invented Thresholds:** NEVER fabricate arbitrary crop stress limits or soil moisture thresholds. All stress triggers must map to documented crop coefficients ($K_c$), maximum allowable depletion (MAD) fractions, or verified agronomic literature (e.g., FAO-56, ICAR packages of practices).
- **Autonomous Actuation:** NEVER trigger automated physical hardware actions (irrigation valves, motors, sprayers) without explicit human confirmation.

## 2. Grounding in Verified Agronomy Sources
- Every advisory generated by the AI advisor must cite verified passages from the local knowledge base (ICAR, FAO-56, regional university packages).
- The deterministic guardrail validator must verify that cited `citation_id` entries exist in the retrieval context. Uncited or fabricated references must result in immediate rejection of the advisory draft.

## 3. Mandatory Abstention Under Incomplete Evidence
- If sensor health is degraded (e.g. stuck value, high drift, stale data), the system must label the alert as `sensor_fault` or `unreliable_data` and **refuse** to generate an irrigation recommendation based on the faulty reading.
- If the field profile lacks critical parameters (e.g. unknown crop growth stage, unknown soil type), or if weather observations contradict sensor trends without explanation, the agent must **abstain** from a definitive recommendation and request a manual field inspection:
  > *"Data is ambiguous due to conflicting sensor trends. Please inspect field #4 manually before taking action."*

## 4. Human-in-the-Loop Approval
- All recommendations delivered to the Farmer PWA or Telegram channel remain in a `PENDING_APPROVAL` state.
- No recommendation is marked as `EXECUTED` until the human operator explicitly clicks **Approve** or **Acknowledge**.
